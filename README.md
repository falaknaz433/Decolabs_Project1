Spice Haven – Restaurant Website

- Introduction

Spice Haven is a modern and interactive restaurant website designed to provide customers with a realistic online restaurant experience. Users can explore the restaurant, discover its specialities, 
browse a complete menu by food categories,
view food images and prices, add dishes to their order, 
manage their cart,
place an order, 
book a table, 
and contact the restaurant.

The website is designed with an attractive restaurant-style interface, smooth animations,
interactive elements,
and a responsive layout that works across desktop,
tablet, and mobile devices.

- Features

- Attractive restaurant landing page
- Restaurant introduction and information
- Specialities and most-loved dishes
- Complete categorized food menu
- Different food categories such as Pakistani, Chinese, BBQ, Biryani, Pizza, Pasta, Salads, Raita, Desserts, and Beverages
- Relevant food images for menu items
- Add to Order and cart functionality
- Increase or decrease item quantity
- Remove items from the cart
- Automatic order total calculation
- Order checkout and confirmation
- Book a Table / reservation form
- Special offers and deals
- Restaurant and food gallery
- Customer reviews
- Contact and restaurant information
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Smooth animations and hover effects
- Interactive menu and ordering experience
