Our Story

A family kitchen that outgrew its walls.

Spice Haven began in 2005 as a six-table karahi house run by chef Imran Aslam and his mother's recipe book. Two decades on, we still grind our garam masala fresh each dawn, source our lamb and chicken from the same butchers, and cook every biryani in small batches — never a steam tray in sight.

About Us

Cooking Karachi's memory, one karahi at a time.

Spice Haven was built on a simple belief: that the best restaurant food should taste like it came from someone's own kitchen — just with a little more fire. Every recipe on our menu traces back to a family table, refined over two decades into what our guests now call "the real thing."

  • Hand-ground spice blends, made fresh every morning
  • Charcoal-fired tandoor and open-flame BBQ pit
  • Locally sourced meat, seafood and produce
  • Four dining rooms across Karachi, one shared kitchen ethos
